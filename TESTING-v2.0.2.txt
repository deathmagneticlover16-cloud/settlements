LAST PAGANS SETTLEMENTS 2.0.2 — MULTIPLAYER TEST CHECKLIST

1. Build and launch
- Server and all clients use only settlementprototype-2.0.2.jar.
- Existing 2.0.x world loads without data loss.

2. Faction creation
- Independent player opens Campaign > Faction.
- Create with no held banner.
- Repeat in a backup with a held patterned banner.
- Confirm the player joins a real Recruits faction and can create/link claims normally.
- Confirm an already-factioned player cannot create another faction.

3. Admin stats
- Test set and add for every supported stat.
- Confirm negative additions do not produce negative food, wealth, housing or contracts.
- Confirm available manpower cannot exceed uncommitted maximum manpower.

4. Character sheet
- Confirm the full model is visible and not cropped to head/shoulders.
- Edit Dynasty and Main House, leave and reopen the server, and verify persistence.
- Check traits, holdings, account text and buttons at the GUI scale used by the server community.

5. Settlement switching
- Give one player access to at least two settlements.
- Cycle Previous/Next in Settlement and Military screens.
- Confirm actions affect the settlement displayed in the title.

6. Multiple claims
- Link three different Recruits claims to one settlement.
- Confirm the claim count becomes 3.
- Stand in each claim and test settlement-dependent behaviour.
- Try linking one of those claims to another settlement; it must be rejected.
- Unlink the middle claim and verify the other two remain.
- Restart and verify all links persist.

7. Layout and banners
- Open Main, Character, Faction, Settlement, Military, Diplomacy, Family and World News.
- Test at GUI scales 2, 3 and 4 where practical.
- Confirm messages do not overlap return buttons.
- Confirm banners are readable without covering titles or controls.
