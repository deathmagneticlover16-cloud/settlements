# Validation

Automated checks are available through `./gradlew regressionChecks` when compilation is desired.

Manual coverage should include treasury flows, foreign-manpower sales, faction formation, direct aid and mercenary upkeep, settlement advancement, Town Square objective relocation, secondary capture zones, explicit trait review, administrator-officiated marriage, explicit Main Seat application, vassal tribute at every preset, independence, faith conversion, and immigration toggling. Confirm that forged player marriage actions and separately editable Main objectives are unavailable.
