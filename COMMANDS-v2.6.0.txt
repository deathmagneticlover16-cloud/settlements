SETTLEMENTS 2.6.0 — COMMANDS

Use in-game command completion for the commands registered by SettlementCommands.
The earlier command reference is retained at docs/legacy/2.5.4/COMMANDS-v2.5.4.txt.
This continuation does not replace that command tree.

Capture designation: Settlement > Manage > Capture (Main/Secondary Pos 1 and Pos 2).
Faction creation with dynamic colors and a designed banner: Campaign > Faction.
Tax slider: Settlement > Treasury.
Research: Campaign > Research (History Stages required).

Treat old command references to settlement Wealth as State Treasury currency.
Capital is a political designation; Major City is the urban/research Tier IV rank.
