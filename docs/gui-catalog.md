# The Culling GUI catalog

The campaign tree has two branches. Each branch exposes related leaves without mixing political and personal controls into local settlement management.

## Navigation branches

- **Settlement Branch** — Settlement Overview, Found Settlement, and Research.
- **Realm Branch** — Character, Family, Faction, Military, Diplomacy, and World News.

Both branches use the same reusable branch-screen implementation.

## Primary workspaces

1. **Campaign Tree** — character and realm summary followed by the Settlement and Realm branch choices.
2. **Character** — portrait, traits, faith, devotion, family access, and inline Main Seat selection.
3. **Settlement** — Overview, Residents, Treasury, Effects, and Management tabs in one screen.
4. **Military** — Forces, Levies, and Mercenaries tabs in one screen, with the company roster as a contextual detail view.
5. **Faction** — setup, banner design, joining, Overview, Members, Settlements, and Ranks in one screen.
6. **Diplomacy** — Relations, Declare Conflict, Proposals, and Active Wars in one screen.
7. **Research** — four development-tier columns, technology details, and the active project.
8. **World News** — filtered campaign history.
9. **World Area Editor** — one in-world selection tool for housing, muster, prayer, and capture geometry.

## Contextual views

- **Family and Marriage** — NPC candidates, player proposals, additional spouses, rejection, and divorce.
- **Found Settlement** — focused settlement-creation form.
- **Housing Areas** — multi-area list attached to Settlement Management, with add, edit, delete, and world-view actions.
- **Mercenary Company Roster** — hiring and contract details attached to the Military workspace.
- **War Aid Ledger** — one-time and recurring manpower, treasury, and food aid attached to a selected war.
- **Peace Terms** — treaty builder attached to a selected war.
- **Wildfire Body Editor** — authorized body customization attached to character creation.

## Persistent overlays

- Housing-area world view
- Capture and area geometry preview
- Expiring campaign alert
- Campaign toast
- Recruits world-map settlement markers

Character creation has two steps: Identity and Body, then Traits. The former standalone preview and Main Seat pages were removed in The Culling.
