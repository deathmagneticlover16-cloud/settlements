LAST PAGANS SETTLEMENTS 2.0.0 — MULTIPLAYER TEST PLAN

PREPARATION
- Back up the world.
- Remove all older settlementprototype JARs.
- Install the same 2.0.0 JAR on server and clients.
- Keep Villager Recruits 1.15.2 installed.
- Test with at least two factions and two players where possible.

1. CHARACTER CREATION
- Join with a fresh/reset character.
- Verify Faith, form of address and title open as real lists.
- Test mouse wheel, arrow keys, Enter and Escape on a dropdown.
- Confirm Orthodox Christianity and all other new faiths appear.
- Verify Preview -> Go Back -> final confirmation.
- Confirm portraits show head and shoulders, not a tiny full body.
- Confirm the character sheet shows trait icons and exact effects on hover.

2. FACTION SCREEN AND RANKS
- Press the normal Recruits faction/team key; it should open Faction, not Diplomacy.
- Confirm Faction and Diplomacy main-menu buttons open different screens.
- As leader, assign a member rank and rename a tier to Jarl/Hersir/Commoner.
- Reopen the menu and reconnect; verify persistence.
- Verify tab/display names use [Rank] Character Name while commands still use account names.
- Verify lower-ranked players cannot change ranks/titles by forged GUI actions.

3. RESIDENT DUPLICATION
- Register one villager repeatedly; population must only change once.
- Use a Settler Contract, then register that spawned villager repeatedly; population must remain unchanged.
- Copy/reuse the same contract ID in Creative; the second redemption must fail.
- Restart the server and repeat the checks.

4. LOCATIONS
- Set a Town Square, Muster Ground and Barracks.
- Verify contract villagers spawn only at the Town Square.
- Verify military recruitment is blocked when the Muster Ground is absent.
- Recruit 10 units and verify they use safe rows around the Muster Ground.
- Link a Barracks chest, issue Return to Barracks and check loaded troops receive the order/upkeep point.

5. ROSTERS
- Create a global default roster and save all seven configured egg types.
- Assign a faction roster and confirm its settlements inherit it.
- Add one settlement-specific override and confirm only that unit type changes.
- Verify a copied template does not reproduce old owner, UUID, position, team or settlement tags.
- Verify the spawned troop is assigned to the current settlement/faction.

6. MERCENARIES AND MORALE
- Mark one held egg as a mercenary, save it to a roster and recruit it.
- Verify an ordinary template does not receive wages.
- Verify the Military screen displays mercenary count and wage obligation.
- Set settlement wealth below the required wage and advance payment cycles.
- Verify Recruits morale falls after missed payments.
- After three missed payments, verify desertion removes settlement/faction tracking.
- Test high/low settlement stability and Barracks rest; morale should gradually move toward different targets rather than jump instantly.

7. DIPLOMACY
- Open Declare Conflict and inspect Raid, Skirmish, Settlement War and Faction War.
- Verify local tiers require a settlement belonging to the selected target faction.
- Verify Faction War targets the whole faction and needs no settlement.
- Verify each tier only offers matching war goals.
- Read the final summary before declaring: attacker, defender, tier, goal and exact target must all be unambiguous.
- Declare each tier in a copied world and verify the Active Wars page records the exact data.
- Verify ordinary friendly/accidental hits do not declare war.
- Verify non-leaders cannot declare war.
- Test NAP, defensive alliance, military alliance, call to war, rejection and peace.
- Verify an expired/ended linked war cannot consume a Call to War proposal as accepted.

8. WORLD NEWS
- Trigger a treaty, war, peace, marriage, settler arrival and mercenary desertion.
- Verify records appear under the correct filter.
- Verify faction-only news is invisible to unrelated players but visible to faction members/admins.
- Restart the server and verify news persists.

9. AI SPOUSES
- Open Family and verify five generated candidates with first/last names, culture, faith, traits and effects.
- Reopen/reconnect and verify the candidates do not reroll.
- Verify refresh is locked until seven Minecraft days have passed.
- Marry a generated spouse and confirm effects appear on settlement/morale summaries.

10. SAVE/RESTART
- Restart after every major system has data.
- Recheck settlements, banners, ranks, rosters, wars, proposals, news, spouse, traits, locations and military UUID tracking.
- Report the complete latest.log and crash report for any failure.
