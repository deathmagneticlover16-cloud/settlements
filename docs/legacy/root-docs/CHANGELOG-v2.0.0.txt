LAST PAGANS SETTLEMENTS 2.0.0 — CAMPAIGN REWORK

ADDED
- Dedicated Faction, Military and World News screens.
- Guided diplomacy declaration flow with exact target, tier, goal and consequence summary.
- Head-and-shoulders portraits throughout the character/campaign interface.
- Real expandable and scrollable dropdown widgets with mouse and keyboard control.
- Expanded religion list including Orthodox Christianity.
- Faction permission tiers and customizable visible titles.
- Dynamic cultural AI spouse generation and seven-day candidate refresh.
- Icon-based character traits and settlement effect explanations.
- Town Square, Muster Ground and Barracks as separate settlement locations.
- Safe row-based military spawning at the Muster Ground.
- Barracks rest/return/upkeep linking.
- Reusable roster inheritance: settlement override -> faction override -> assigned roster -> global default -> fallback.
- Selective mercenary tags, wage intervals, missed-payment morale penalties and desertion.
- Villager Recruits morale integration.
- Public/faction world-news records and category filters.
- Raid and Skirmish conflict tiers.
- Active-war ally selection and Call to War controls, with defensive-alliance restrictions.

CHANGED
- Recruits' faction key now opens the dedicated Faction screen.
- Faction and Diplomacy buttons no longer point to the same page.
- Military no longer redirects to the Settlement screen.
- Military units no longer spawn at the Town Square.
- Character names may include the faction rank prefix while preserving real usernames/UUIDs.
- Settlement banner rendering keeps the corrected large scales from V1.1.1.
- Legacy Pagan/Christian character faith values migrate to the expanded system.

FIXED/HARDENED
- Central resident registration remains idempotent for manual and contract villagers.
- Contract IDs remain single-use and server-authoritative.
- Template spawning strips stale UUID, owner, position, team and settlement metadata.
- Diplomacy and management permissions are checked server-side.
- Invalid target-settlement/faction and war-goal/tier combinations are rejected server-side.
- Calls to wars are not marked accepted when the linked war has already ended.
- Barracks loaded from older data receive safe default radius/capacity values.
- Mercenary payment schedules catch up safely after recruits or chunks have been unloaded.
- Settlement effect icons safely fall back to a book for invalid item IDs.

REMOVED FROM ROADMAP
- Tactical bird's-eye command mode.
- Total War-style unit-card overview.
