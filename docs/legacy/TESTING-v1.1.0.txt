LAST PAGANS SETTLEMENTS V1.1.0 — TEST PLAN
Use a copied world and two test accounts/factions where diplomacy testing requires both sides.

1. BUILD AND STARTUP
• Remove every older settlementprototype JAR.
• Build V1.1.0 and install it on client and server.
• Confirm the old world loads and existing settlement data remains.

2. CHARACTER FLOW
• Reset a test character as an operator.
• Rejoin and confirm character creation opens.
• Open Faith, Gender/address, and Title dropdowns; select entries from the expanded lists.
• Press Preview Character.
• Use Go Back, change a value, preview again, then Confirm Character.
• Confirm the character sheet opens and creation does not reopen after reconnecting.
• Press J from normal gameplay.
• Open Character from the Campaign menu and verify Return to Main appears.
• Confirm the 3D skin portrait and RP name display.

3. KEYBINDS AND ORIGINAL RECRUITS DIPLOMACY LOCKOUT
• Press M: Campaign menu opens.
• Press N: selected settlement opens.
• Press the normal Recruits faction/team key: Campaign Diplomacy opens instead.
• Attempt to reach Recruits diplomacy, embargo, and treaty screens through normal Recruits routes or a messenger. They should redirect, and server relation-change packets should not take effect.

4. DAMAGE DOES NOT DECLARE WAR
• Put two players in different neutral factions.
• Hit the other player and/or recruit.
• Confirm Recruits does not permanently switch both factions to Enemy solely from the hit.
• Individual self-defence may still occur; the formal relation must remain unchanged.

5. FORMAL DIPLOMACY
• As faction leader A, offer a non-aggression pact to faction B.
• As faction leader B, accept the first proposal.
• Confirm both Campaign screens show NON_AGGRESSION and Recruits remains Neutral.
• Repeat with defensive or military alliance and confirm Recruits becomes Ally.
• Break the treaty and confirm the relation returns to Neutral and reliability falls.
• Verify a normal faction member cannot perform leader-only diplomacy.

6. FORMAL WAR AND PEACE
• Declare a Raid or Faction War from faction A against B.
• Confirm both Campaign screens show an active war and Recruits becomes Enemy.
• Offer peace from one side and accept from the other.
• Confirm the war ends and Recruits returns to Neutral.
• While allied, start a war and send Call Ally; accept on the allied faction and confirm it joins the conflict.

7. MARRIAGE
• Open Family from the Campaign menu.
• Marry one placeholder NPC and verify the spouse appears on Family and Character sheets.
• End the marriage.
• Use /character marry <player> with two unmarried online characters.
• Accept in the target's Family screen and verify both character sheets show the other spouse.
• Test Reject and Divorce.

8. SETTLEMENT BANNERS
• Hold a patterned banner and press Set held banner or run /settlements banner sethand <id>.
• Verify the large local banner is clearly visible.
• For a faction settlement, verify the smaller faction allegiance banner also appears.
• Switch to faction banner and then no banner.
• Test an independent settlement with a custom banner.
• Restart and verify banner persistence.

9. MILITARY ROSTERS
• Create a default roster.
• Configure and hold a spawn egg for each unit type, then save it with roster sethand.
• Assign the default roster globally and recruit each unit type from the Military tab.
• Confirm the entity type/equipment/health configuration matches the egg.
• Confirm each professional is unhired, belongs to the settlement faction, and can be hired normally.
• Confirm copied owner, old team, old group, and old settlement information do not survive.
• Configure a weaker levy egg and raise a levy. Confirm the Levyman name and configured low health.
• Add one settlement-specific override and verify only that settlement uses it.

10. MAP MARKERS
• Open the Recruits world map.
• Verify every settlement with a town square appears at the correct location.
• Hover Village, Town, and Capital markers and check name, faction, population, and stability.
• Verify other dimensions do not render on the current dimension map.

11. REGRESSION: RESIDENTS AND CONTRACTS
• Register the same villager repeatedly. Population must only rise once.
• Spawn a contract settler, then repeatedly run registervillager on it. Population must not increase again.
• Copy/reuse the same Settler Contract in Creative. The unique contract ID must redeem only once.

12. PERSISTENCE
Restart the server and verify:
• Campaign diplomacy, wars, treaties, reliability, marriage, rosters, templates, banner modes, characters, settlements, residents, resources, MP, and map markers remain.
