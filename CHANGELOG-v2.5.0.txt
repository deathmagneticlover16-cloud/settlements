SETTLEMENTS 2.5.0 — CHANGELOG

RESEARCH / HISTORY STAGES
- Added Campaign > Research screen.
- Added one-active-project-per-polity technology research.
- Added optional reflection-only History Stages 5.4.0+ integration.
- Added dynamic History Stages technology list, prerequisites, icons/metadata, durations and unlock synchronization.
- Research uses active server ticks rather than Minecraft day length.
- Added persistent research progress/completion SavedData.
- Added independent-polity research shared across an independent ruler's holdings.
- Added research migration into a newly founded/joined political faction.
- Added Scholarly trait, default +15% research speed.
- Added configurable Prosperity research-speed bonus.
- Added /campaign research fallback command.

INDEPENDENT SETTLEMENT ARCHITECTURE
- Added hidden technical Recruits backing factions for independent rulers.
- Independent settlements can now own/use Recruits claims and military teams without pretending to be full political factions in Settlements diplomacy.
- Backing factions are filtered from normal political faction/diplomacy interfaces.
- Claims and loaded managed units migrate from the technical backing faction to a real faction when appropriate.
- Added safe transition logic around Recruits' faction-leader membership checks.
- Full faction creation is now configurable progression requiring a developed independent settlement and settlement Wealth.
- Added clearer faction-creation screen explanation of development requirements.

MERCENARY COMPANIES
- Added dedicated Military > Mercenary Rosters GUI.
- Added config/settlements-mercenary-rosters.json generated defaults and live timestamp reload.
- Added /settlements admin mercenary reload_rosters.
- Added named global companies with custom item icons, troop compositions, template rosters, hire costs, upkeep intervals and starting loyalty.
- Added persistent employer trust/loyalty.
- Added paid-company refusal to defect at high effective loyalty.
- Added loyalty-based buyout premiums and bidder Reliability price modifiers.
- Added company-level upkeep, missed-payment states and desertion.
- Company states: Paid, Discontented, Unpaid, Deserting.
- Paying wages improves trust and faction Reliability; missed wages reduce both.
- Honorable paid contract termination improves company trust.
- Added safe retirement of unloaded stale company entities after buyout/desertion.
- Added company unit death tracking.
- Added company totals/status into the normal Military summary.
- Added /campaign mercenaries fallback command.

FOUNDATION CARRIED FORWARD FROM 2.4.1
- Canonical Settlements identity; no active Prototype naming.
- Taxation, #settlements:currency, first settlers, Reliability display, Main Seat, no Dynasty, independent aid frequencies, protected-polity security, villager population cleanup/confinement, faction palette correction, dropdown top layering and Manage GUI restructuring.
