SETTLEMENTS 2.5.2 — CHANGELOG

POPULATION / GROWTH
- Replaced the fixed 5-minute Settler Contract cadence with dynamic Settler Attraction.
- Stability, free Housing and Food jointly accelerate or slow eligible settlement growth.
- Added configurable fastest/slowest contract intervals; defaults 2–12 active-server minutes.
- Residents now displays attraction %, blockers and live countdown.
- Removed the obsolete fixed settlerContractIntervalMinutes setting.

CITY UPKEEP
- Added a linkable City Upkeep Chest/Container.
- Edible items are automatically converted into Food to maintain a configurable multi-day stockpile target.
- Added maximum consumed items per maintenance sweep.
- Added Locations and Treasury GUI controls/status for upkeep.

BARDS
- Added Bard designation for nearby managed recruits.
- Bards stationed in settlement territory contribute settlement-wide morale; campaigning Bards provide a configurable nearby field-morale aura, with capped stacking.
- Bard performing at the Town Square with a tagged instrument gives configurable daily Stability.
- Added #settlements:bard_instruments item tag; goat horn is the default member.

TRAITS / PLAGUE / RELIABILITY
- Healthy now reduces natural Plague chance.
- Added natural configurable Plague occurrence using the existing Plague modifier/news/toast system.
- Reserved now scales all Dynamic Reliability gains and losses for the faction ruler.
- Reliability feedback now reports the actual Reserved-adjusted divorce penalty.

ROMAN SUPPORT
- Added Roman Paganism to valid character/spouse religions.
- Added Roman generated spouse culture/name pool.
- Roman Pagan rulers preferentially receive Roman spouse candidates.

RESEARCH / HISTORY STAGES
- Server now enforces History Stages minimum/exact pedestal tier requirements.
- Added polity Research Tier based on highest developed holding: Village 1, Town 2, City-State 3, Capital 4.
- Rebuilt Research GUI into a Total-War-inspired multi-column technology tree.
- Added live server refresh of progress, timer and technology availability while Research is open.
- Completed/available/locked states now refresh without reopening the menu.
- History Stages completion bridge now refreshes stage cache, posts IndividualUnlocked, syncs the individual-stage packet, plays a level-up sound and sends a Settlements technology toast.
- Added dependency-depth ordering and tier/prerequisite details.

MILITARY POPULATION
- Fixed named Mercenary Roster soldiers being registered as both professional and mercenary units during spawn.
- Added load-time overlap repair.
- Named company soldiers count once in military population and remain excluded from professional-manpower usage.

FACTION FOUNDING
- Added master factionFounding.enabled configuration option.
- Existing development and Wealth thresholds remain configurable.

GUI / QUALITY
- Re-spaced Manage > Locations after adding City Upkeep Chest to prevent label/button overlap.
- Settlement/Military snapshots now expose upkeep/Bard state.
- Network protocol bumped to 33 for the changed snapshot formats.

CARRIED FORWARD FROM 2.5.1
- Active-time research and one project per polity.
- Scholarly + Prosperity research speed.
- Hidden independent backing factions.
- Named Mercenary Rosters and loyalty/poaching.
- Taxation, #settlements:currency, first settlers, Main Seat, Reliability, no Dynasty, claim-bound residents, dynamic relations and diplomacy hardening.
