SETTLEMENTS + HISTORY STAGES — RESEARCH TREE GUIDE
Target integration: History Stages 5.4.0 / Minecraft 1.20.1

WHAT SETTLEMENTS USES
Settlements treats History Stages INDIVIDUAL stages as technologies.
Do not create the campaign technology tree as Global stages if you want Settlements to manage it.

The division of responsibility is:
- History Stages defines the technology: display name, icon, research time, locked items/tags and prerequisite stage IDs.
- Settlements displays those technologies as a polity technology tree.
- Settlements enforces one active project per polity and advances its own campaign research timer while the server is running.
- When the project completes, Settlements grants the corresponding History Stages individual stage to the polity's eligible players.
- History Stages then enforces the actual per-player content locks/unlocks.

SIMPLE ARMOUR TREE
The easiest reliable setup is three individual stages:

    Early Armour  ->  Improved Armour  ->  Advanced Armour
    armor_tier_1      armor_tier_2         armor_tier_3

1. Open the History Stages in-game editor as an OP.
2. Create INDIVIDUAL stage `armor_tier_1`.
   - Display name: Early Armour
   - Choose an armour/item icon.
   - Set the research time you want.
   - Put the Tier 1 equipment/items or, preferably, your Tier 1 equipment tag in its locked Items/Tags section.
3. Create INDIVIDUAL stage `armor_tier_2`.
   - Display name: Improved Armour
   - Lock the Tier 2 equipment/items/tag.
   - In Dependencies, add an INDIVIDUAL STAGE prerequisite pointing to `armor_tier_1`.
4. Create INDIVIDUAL stage `armor_tier_3`.
   - Display name: Advanced Armour
   - Lock the Tier 3 equipment/items/tag.
   - Add an INDIVIDUAL STAGE prerequisite pointing to `armor_tier_2`.
5. Reload/reopen as required by History Stages, then open Settlements -> Research.

Settlements 2.5.4 reads those dependency IDs and lays them out as a visible tree with arrows. `armor_tier_2` cannot be started until `armor_tier_1` is in the polity's completed research set, and the same applies to Tier 3.

IMPORTANT: EQUIPMENT TIER VS SETTLEMENT RESEARCH TIER
History Stages has `min_pedestal_tier` / `pedestal_tier_mode` fields for its own Research Pedestal tiers.
Settlements reuses those values as a SETTLEMENT DEVELOPMENT gate:
- 1 = Village
- 2 = Town
- 3 = City-State
- 4 = Capital

This is separate from "Tier 1 armour / Tier 2 armour".

If you ONLY want the technology chain to control armour progression, leave every armour technology at minimum pedestal tier 1 and use the individual-stage dependencies to make the chain.

If you ALSO want Advanced Armour to require a developed settlement, for example:
- Early Armour: min pedestal tier 1
- Improved Armour: min pedestal tier 2
- Advanced Armour: min pedestal tier 3

Then a player needs BOTH the preceding technology AND a polity with a sufficiently developed settlement.

WHY INDIVIDUAL STAGES
Settlements grants research to players as History Stages individual stages. This allows "researched FOR YOU" content locking instead of automatically unlocking an era for the entire server.

For equipment progression, prefer locking the actual armour item IDs or an armour item tag in the individual stage. That gives History Stages a concrete per-player lock to enforce.

NATIVE HISTORY STAGES RESEARCH VS SETTLEMENTS RESEARCH
History Stages normally researches through Research Scrolls and Research Pedestals.
Settlements is an alternative campaign-facing research driver: its GUI/timer completes the technology and then grants the same individual stage.
You do not need to make players run the History Stages pedestal as well unless you deliberately want both systems available.

DEPENDENCY LIMIT FOR SETTLEMENTS 2.5.4
For a clean strategy-game tree, use History Stages `individual_stage` dependencies between your technologies.
History Stages also supports non-stage requirements such as XP, kills, statistics and scoreboard conditions, but Settlements' campaign tree is currently designed around stage-to-stage prerequisites and should not be used as the UI for those extra dependency types.

TREE DESIGN EXAMPLE
You can branch exactly like a Total War technology tree:

                      -> Heavy Infantry
                     /
Basic Smithing -> Iron Working -> Improved Armour -> Advanced Armour
                     \
                      -> Improved Weapons

Each arrow is simply an Individual Stage dependency. Multiple later stages can depend on the same earlier stage.

For the least version-specific configuration work, use the History Stages in-game editor rather than hand-writing JSON.
