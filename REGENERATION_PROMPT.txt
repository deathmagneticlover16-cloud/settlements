CURRENT DESIGN DECISIONS
- Capture zones: Village=1 Main; Town=1 Main+1 Secondary; larger city=1 Main+2 Secondaries.
- Capture zones need minimum size, spacing, no overlap/nesting, must belong to linked claims.
- Secondary capture should be exposed to RTS for morale effects; Main + secondaries leads to occupation, not immediate legal transfer.
- State Treasury is the spendable polity fund; settlement Wealth as a separate stockpile is being retired.
- Taxation should be a real draggable slider, not Low/Normal/High buttons.
- Research tiers: I Village, II Town, III City, IV Major City. Capital alone must never grant Tier IV.
- Administrative Capacity is deferred to future Government/Faction overhaul.
- Dye Depot 1.3.0 extends DyeColor with extra colors; Settlements should enumerate DyeColor.values() dynamically and remain Dye Depot optional.
- Faction creation target UI: faction name, dynamic faction color, dynamic unit color, government type, capital, loom-like banner editor.
