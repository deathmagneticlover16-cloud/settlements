SETTLEMENTS 2.6.0 — TEST PLAN

AUTOMATED
Run gradlew.bat build (or regressionChecks alone). Checks exercise actual mapped
Minecraft classes: banner sanitization, capture geometry/control persistence,
treasury accounting, dye-name persistence, capital/rank research separation,
mercenary template-state sanitization, and network round trips with more than
256 capture objectives. Character-body checks cover finite/range validation,
Male breast disabling, NBT/network round trips and legacy records without Body.

MANUAL — FRESH TEST WORLD, MATCHING CLIENT/SERVER BUILDS
1. Found an independent settlement in linked Recruits claims. Check its initial
   Main zone, founding cooldown and shared treasury. If the initial footprint
   cannot fit, designate a valid Main with Manage > Capture.
2. Advance Village -> Town -> City -> Major City. Town requires population 100,
   Prosperity 35, Stability 45; City 250/55/55. Major City defaults to population
   500, Stability 65 and Prosperity 70 (server configurable). All require housing
   for registered villagers and the next tier's capture objectives.
3. Verify a Capital at Village/Town/City rank does not grant Tier IV research.
   Verify an established Major City does, including a non-capital Major City.
4. Faction creation: enter a name, select government/capital and colors, design
   1–6 pattern layers, return to Identity and resize the window. Preserve drafts.
   Force a validation error (insufficient funds or duplicate name), then correct it
   without losing the banner. Confirm treasury cost and transfer happen once.
5. Repeat colors/banner tests with Dye Depot 1.3.0 on both sides, including teal,
   navy and another added dye. Restart and verify saved names, banner and map color.
   Recruits unit/chat colors are nearest-palette approximations. Repeat without
   Dye Depot in a separate fresh world. Full Forge mixin/render testing is manual.
6. Drag taxation to 50, 83 and 150%; verify the committed value, income and effects.
   Use keyboard arrows and verify the value persists after reopening.
7. Deposit/withdraw physical currency, recruit professionals, hire mercenaries,
   process upkeep, send aid and resolve treasury payments. Two settlements in one
   polity must show the same balance; no action should spend a separate local wallet.
   Interact with every named-company recruit and open Recruits' debug controls.
   They must move normally and obey orders. End the contract and verify every unit
   disappears without floating/running in place and the Recruits unit count drops.
   In a copy of an affected pre-fix world, leave the company loaded for 30 seconds
   (or reload its chunks) and verify its surviving units become interactive again.
   Confirm the group manager contains one group named for the company (for example
   Free Spears) and that every company soldier is a member. Rehire the same company
   and verify Settlements reuses the group instead of making a duplicate.
8. Capture placement: reject undersized, unlinked, nested, overlapping and too-close
   zones. Replace Main using an overlapping old footprint. Add a spare Secondary,
   remove the old one, and verify tier minimums remain satisfied. Editing must fail
   during war/occupation. Claim unlinking must fail while it contains an objective.
9. In a war targeting the defender settlement, take secondaries then Main. Confirm
   occupation and a single event/reward per capture. Contest/leave/re-enter an
   already captured objective; this must not repeatedly add capture war score.
10. Retake a Secondary and then the Main. Defenders must be able to liberate the
    Main even while its attack prerequisites are locked. End the war: occupation
    clears and all objectives recover. Repeat across save/reload; spectators must
    not capture. Legal settlement ownership must remain separate from occupation.
11. Open all campaign tabs at multiple GUI scales. Test dropdown overlays and long
    names; verify research edges, Major City messaging and no Bard controls.
12. Create a Male character. The Boobs Yes/Edit controls must be disabled and the
    final Wildfire bust must be zero. Repeat with Female and Other, testing both No
    and Yes. In Yes, change every value in Wildfire's breast editor, return to the
    Settlements creator, preview, go back through traits and submit. Draft values
    must survive each page, while the live Wildfire state must not change before
    final submission or after cancelling/leaving the editor.
13. Force a final-submission error (duplicate/invalid name or invalid traits) after
    editing the body. No body value may commit. Correct the error and submit; then
    verify the saved appearance immediately, after relog, restart, respawn and a
    dimension change. Existing characters from before this build must remain
    unmanaged until reset/recreated.
14. With two clients, watch a managed character submit and verify the observer sees
    the same body. Attempt to open Wildfire wardrobe/settings/editor screens and to
    send a changed Wildfire body; managed/onboarding characters must be blocked and
    corrected. Run the admin character reset and verify editing is unlocked until
    the replacement character is submitted.
