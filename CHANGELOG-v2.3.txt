LAST PAGANS SETTLEMENTS 2.3.0 — CAMPAIGN OVERHAUL

SECURITY / MULTIPLAYER
- Strict server-side settlement visibility, contribution, and management permissions.
- Foreign-faction settlement management is blocked even with forged client packets.
- Settlement switcher only exposes settlements the viewer may access.
- Independent settlement ownership limit: 2.
- Full admin player/character/subsystem reset and inspection tools.

SETTLEMENTS
- Multiple Recruits claims can be linked to one settlement.
- Claims GUI: Link Current, Unlink Current, confirmation for Unlink All.
- GUI settlement creation, rename, ownership transfer and point/region setup.
- Physical housing regions (pos1/pos2); valid bed heads contribute housing.
- Physical Muster Ground regions; horizontal usable area limits levy size.
- Town Square, Barracks, Muster Ground and Prayer Site separated.
- Dynamic Prosperity driven primarily by a rolling 7-day average of actual wealth deposited into the settlement, with migration-safe fallback for old saves.
- Automatic Village -> Town progression; independent high-tier settlements can become City-States.
- Civic Pride and icon-based active settlement modifier display.

CHARACTERS / FAMILY
- Full live 3D player portrait restored.
- Dynasty and Main House can be edited later from the character sheet.
- Three-round trait-card character creation: 3 random choices per round, choose 1, total 3 traits; one reroll.
- Expanded leader traits including charismatic/military/levy-oriented traits.
- Generated AI spouse candidates: at least 5 female + 5 male, cultural-ish first/surnames, faiths, traits and effects.
- Male/Female/Any candidate filter, rejection history and 7-day refresh.
- Player-to-player marriage and Marriage Alliances.
- Player spouses contribute their live character trait effects to the ruler's Capital/City-State, matching AI spouse settlement effects.
- Seven-day minimum before voluntary divorce; repeated divorce creates Marital Scandal.
- Spouse settlement effects apply to capitals/City-States.
- Islam multi-spouse easter egg: up to four total spouses; additional NPC-wife settlement effects diminish to 50%, 25%, then 10%.

FACTIONS
- Dedicated faction management screen.
- Faction creator restored inside Settlements.
- Recruits team color + unit color selection.
- Held banner is mandatory; blank/unpatterned banners are rejected.
- Faction ranks/titles with permission tiers.
- Faction treasury, including settlement <-> treasury transfer controls.
- Protected admin factions/settlements.

DIPLOMACY / WAR
- Guided diplomacy redesign.
- Settlement War declaration: choose YOUR settlement -> TARGET settlement -> Casus Belli.
- Settlement Wars are settlement-vs-settlement; parent factions begin as observers.
- Defender parent: Ignore / Send Aid / Join War. Attacker parent: Send Aid / Join War.
- Explicit escalation to Faction-v-Settlement and finally Faction-v-Faction.
- One-time or recurring Food / Wealth / Manpower war aid.
- War Goal renamed Casus Belli; Custom CB limited to 100 chars; Holy War added.
- Political relations, religious compatibility and reliability shown separately.
- Reliability changes from actual diplomatic conduct rather than random character trait selection.
- Reliability now rewards answering calls to war, completing reparations, and honouring forced non-aggression periods; treaty-breaking, aggressive declarations and missed reparations reduce it.
- Recruits diplomacy relation is synchronized and verified for formal faction wars.
- Custom diplomatic toasts for declarations, escalation, aid, treaties, peace, marriage alliances and other major changes.
- War score and ±90 capitulation threshold; battlefield recruit casualties alter score.

PEACE TREATIES
- Structured peace offers with acceptance/rejection.
- Lump wealth.
- Manpower transfer (temporary Foreign Soldiers consequences on receiver).
- Daily reparations for a duration.
- Forced non-aggression pact.
- Forced faith conversion.
- Whole settlement transfer.
- Individual linked-claim transfer into a receiving settlement.
- Recognition of independence.
- Capitulated side cannot reject terms from the dominant side.

RELIGION
- Catholic Christianity, Orthodox Christianity, Finnic/Baltic/Norse/Slavic Paganism, Islam, Judaism and Other.
- Personal Devotion / faith standing.
- Admin-defined Holy Site regions.
- Settlement Prayer Site regions.
- Holy-site/prayer devotion gains.
- Religious compatibility in diplomacy.
- Local Religious Unrest modifiers for politically allied but strongly incompatible faiths.
- Holy War Casus Belli and faith-conversion peace term.

MILITARY / LEVIES
- Reusable global roster, faction roster and settlement overrides from full configured spawn-egg ItemStacks.
- Professionals remain unhired for faction members to hire normally.
- Levies spawn already owned by the player who raised them; Recruits hiring limit is therefore not used for levy spawning.
- Automatic/reused Recruits Levy group when API permits.
- Default deployed limits: 300/player, 500/faction.
- Levy size constrained by manpower and Muster Ground area.
- Staggered levy arrival; larger levy = longer mobilization, approximately 4 sec/unit up to 15 minutes default.
- Long real-world cooldown with emergency hostile-action relief.
- Default levy health: 12 under 100, 10 at 100+; configurable.
- Levy recruit level cap default 3; global Recruits level override default 5; configurable/removable.
- Return Levy action.
- Multi-day service unrest stages: Extended Service, Weary Levies, Levy Exhaustion, Popular Outrage.
- Casualty percentage produces escalating settlement mourning penalties, up to Levy Massacre.
- Levy Tradition: repeated meaningful levy service with surviving veterans gradually gives future levies a small capped starting-XP bonus; immediate returns and near-total massacres do not farm it.
- Leader traits can alter levy health, XP, morale and muster time.
- Villager Recruits morale is used rather than a competing morale system.

MERCENARIES
- Rewritten persistent mercenary contract ledger.
- Template-marked mercenaries register even when units unload.
- Faction treasury pays faction mercenaries; independent mercs use home-settlement wealth.
- Military summaries show persistent mercenary count/wage data.
- Missed payments progress toward desertion; deserters become neutral when loaded.
- Admin can force a payment check for testing.

WORLD NEWS / UI
- Separate World News screen remains integrated with major campaign events.
- Character/settlement traits and active modifiers use clearer icon-based summaries.
- More campaign actions moved out of commands and into GUIs.
- Screen layouts substantially reorganized to reduce 2.0.x overlap.
- Settlement War peace negotiation now respects escalation state: parent factions cannot negotiate as belligerents until they explicitly join, and pure settlement wars cannot quietly impose faction-wide reparations/NAP terms or browse unrelated faction territory in the treaty builder.
- Peace Terms are now available to actual settlement managers in local wars (including independents), while joined faction leadership can negotiate faction territory after escalation; observer parent factions cannot use the treaty UI before joining.
- Capitulation alerts now trigger only when war score actually crosses ±90, notify the real participating settlement/faction side (not an observing parent faction), and create a major World News entry without repeated toast spam on every later casualty.
- Returning a bloodied levy now immediately notifies settlement managers of the casualty modifier; losses of 50%+ create Military news, and 75%+ catastrophes become public major news.
- Settlements' replacement faction creator now preserves Recruits' configured creation requirements/cost, rejects duplicate display names/banners, and verifies that Recruits actually created the faction before reporting success.
- Ending a local Settlement War no longer resets diplomacy between parent factions that never joined the conflict; only actual faction belligerents/supporters are synchronized back to Neutral.
- Settlement-war Join/Ignore responses now notify the real political sides, including independent settlement managers; a defending parent that chooses Ignore visibly informs both local settlements instead of changing state silently.
- Admin levy/full-player resets now perform real military cleanup: loaded levies are removed, unloaded stragglers are retired so they cannot reappear, partial mobilisation reservations are cleared/refunded, and phantom deployment records are removed.
- Legacy one-click faction peace is now server-blocked for Settlement Wars (including forged/old packets); local conflicts must use the structured Peace Terms system so observer parents cannot silently end them.
- A levy annihilated in the field now immediately triggers the full Levy Massacre notification and a major public Military news entry; the catastrophe no longer waits for a Return Levy action that can never happen.

FINAL HARDENING
- Recurring Settlement War aid now has a persistent player-facing ledger, can be cancelled by the donating parent faction, and remains visible to the aided settlement.
- Settlement-war action buttons were rearranged so Ignore / Aid / Join / Peace no longer overlap on the defending-parent view.
- Peace Terms no longer recursively resolves attacker/defender ownership when opened by local settlement managers.
- Manpower aid validates the recipient's remaining manpower capacity before deducting donor manpower, preventing silent resource loss.
- Independent rulers no longer create meaningless blank-faction diplomacy records merely by opening the Diplomacy screen.
- Network protocol advanced to 24 for the expanded diplomacy snapshot.
